How It Works

A calm, organized process from your first quote to the final walk-through.

Five steps to a spotless space

1

Request your quote

Use our online calculator to select your service, home size and add-ons. You see your estimate instantly and receive it by email.

  • Takes about a minute
  • No obligation
2

Choose your date

Pick your preferred day and arrival window, and tell us how we’ll access your space and whether you have pets.

3

We confirm the details

Our team reviews your request and contacts you to confirm availability, your final price and any special instructions.

4

We clean, top to bottom

Our uniformed team arrives prepared and works through a room-by-room checklist, starting high and finishing with the floors.

  • Dusting and detailing
  • Kitchens and bathrooms
  • Floors last, so nothing is tracked back
5

Final walk-through

Before we leave, we check our work against the checklist. If something isn’t right, tell us and we’ll make it right.

Get ready in a few minutes

A little preparation helps us spend our time on cleaning rather than tidying.

  • Put away personal items, papers and valuables.
  • Clear countertops and floors where possible.
  • Secure pets or let us know how to meet them.
  • Share parking, entry codes or building instructions.
  • Tell us your priorities so we focus where it matters.

What we bring

Supplies

Cleaning products and cloths for every surface, organized on our service cart.

Detail tools

Dusters, soft brushes and microfiber for corners, edges and delicate décor.

Professionalism

A uniformed, courteous team that treats your space with care.
